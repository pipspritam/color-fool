# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated / Effective Date:** September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Color Fool** ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by **Pritam Mondal** (`com.pritampips.color_fool`), handles user information.

We are committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. **Color Fool is designed with a strict privacy-first principle: we do not collect, store, track, or sell any personal data.**

---

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect

Color Fool does not require any user registration, sign-in, or account creation. We do NOT collect:
- Personal Identifiable Information (such as your real name, email address, phone number, or physical address).
- Precise or approximate geographical location.
- Contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data.
- Financial, payment, or banking information.
- Device identifiers or advertising tracking IDs.

---

## 2. Multiplayer Gameplay & Transient Data

When you play **Multiplayer Mode**:
- **Display Names**: You choose a temporary nickname (e.g., "Golumolu") solely to identify players in the live room. This nickname is transient and is not saved to any persistent user profile.
- **Real-Time Game Actions**: Color guesses, scores, and room codes are communicated in real time via encrypted WebSocket channels (provided by Supabase). This data is ephemeral—it exists only during active matches and is not used for tracking or profiling.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

The App uses a minimal set of trusted third-party infrastructure solely to deliver gameplay functionality:
- **Supabase Realtime**: Powers real-time multiplayer message exchange over secure, encrypted channels (WSS). Supabase does not collect personal data from Color Fool users.
  - [Supabase Privacy Policy](https://supabase.com/privacy)

We do not use any third-party advertising networks (no Google AdMob, Unity Ads, etc.) or tracking analytics SDKs.

---

## 4. Offline Solo Play

Solo gameplay runs 100% locally on your device. All color calculations, timer logic, and scoring formulas operate entirely offline without transmitting any data over the internet.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our App is safe for all audiences, including children. Because we do not collect any personal information whatsoever, we do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.

---

## 6. Security

We value your trust in using our App. All multiplayer network communications utilize industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS/WSS) encryption.

---

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
